Verifiable Delay Function

Evaluate sequential modular squaring, construct a Wesolowski quotient proof, and verify it without repeating the delay computation. Runs entirely in your browser.

Educational implementation only. The sample modulus has known factors and the challenge is 64 bits. This is not an audited or production-secure VDF. Do not use for randomness beacons, lotteries, consensus, or security-sensitive applications.

Parameters

Demo: 1,000,003 × 1,000,033. Supply an odd modulus up to 4096 bits.

Must be coprime to N and between 2 and N−2.

1–10,000 sequential squarings; runtime depends on your device and modulus size.

How the proof works

  1. Evaluate: compute y = x^(2^T) mod N through T sequential modular squarings.
  2. Challenge: derive a transcript-bound 64-bit prime ℓ using SHA-256.
  3. Prove: compute π = x^floor(2^T / ℓ) mod N by binary long division of the exponent.
  4. Verify: confirm π^ℓ × x^r mod N = y, where r = 2^T mod ℓ, and recompute the challenge from the transcript.
